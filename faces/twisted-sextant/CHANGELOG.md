### Added
- First release: Sextant with the corner complications turned to run along their gauges (values at the rim, icons inward), the 22 metrics the face can read in every editable slot (including sleep, resting heart rate, training, altitude, air pressure, compass, moon phase and today's forecast), and weather fixed at the top.
