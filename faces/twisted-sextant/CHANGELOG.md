## [0.1.0] - YYYY-MM-DD

### Added
- First release: Sextant with the corner complications turned to run along their gauges (values at the rim, icons inward), the 22 metrics the face can read in every editable slot (including sleep, resting heart rate, training, altitude, air pressure, compass, moon phase and today's forecast), and weather fixed at the top. Defaults: calories, heart rate, distance, steps and stand. The diagonal compass markers sit at the middle of the corner gauges (43.5° rather than 45.5°), so each points at its turned value.
